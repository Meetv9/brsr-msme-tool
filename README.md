## About Ecosetu

Ecosetu helps Indian MSMEs prepare BRSR-ready sustainability reports in 60 minutes — without the consultancy fees.

**Built for:**
- Sole Proprietorships, Partnerships, LLPs, and Private Limited businesses
- MSMEs that need to disclose ESG performance for bank loans, export contracts, or government schemes
- Companies preferring plain-language tools over jargon-heavy enterprise platforms

**Features:**
- 9 NGRBC Principles covered (SEBI BRSR format)
- Quick Mode (10-15 questions per principle) + Full Mode
- Auto-calculated scores, emissions, and intensity metrics
- BRSR-audit-ready PDF report generation
- Tier-aware: Hides irrelevant questions based on business type
